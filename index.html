Kaprekar's Routine → 6174

Take any number whose digits are not all the same. Sort its digits from largest to smallest, sort them again from smallest to largest, and subtract. Repeat with the answer. Four-digit numbers always land on 6174 within 7 steps; three-digit numbers land on 495.
